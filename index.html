01 / About

Curiosity that turned into a discipline.

I was born in 2005 and developed a strong interest in computers in 2020 — the beginning of my journey into technology. What started as curiosity quickly grew into a passion for understanding how systems work, how they can be secured, and how vulnerabilities are found and mitigated.

In 2024 I began my professional IT journey, focusing on cybersecurity — particularly ethical hacking and web security analysis. Since then I've built hands-on experience identifying and analyzing security weaknesses in web applications through multiple vulnerability assessments, and I've developed the habit of documenting findings clearly: risk, impact, and recommended fix.

I'm growing toward penetration testing, digital forensics, and advanced threat analysis — with the goal of becoming a professional who doesn't just find vulnerabilities, but helps organizations build systems that hold up.
